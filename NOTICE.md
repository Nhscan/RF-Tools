# Credits and source provenance

RF Tools is maintained by Nhscan and developed against [Nhscan/unleashed-firmware-Nhscan](https://github.com/Nhscan/unleashed-firmware-Nhscan).

It uses the firmware's Sub-GHz decoders, encoders, radio-device abstraction, GUI, storage, and notification APIs. Its scene structure and several radio/analyzer patterns follow the stock Sub-GHz app and Unleashed firmware implementation. Credit belongs to the Flipper Devices, Unleashed/DarkFlippers, and protocol contributors for their respective work.

This app is not an official Flipper Devices product. The GNU GPL v3 text is included as LICENSE; upstream notices and credits remain applicable.
